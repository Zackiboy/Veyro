FAQ

What is Veyro One?

Veyro One is a 20-minute visual countdown timer. Instead of relying on a digital clock, it gives you a clear visual representation of your remaining time.

Is Veyro One available yet?

Veyro One is currently in development. Join the waitlist to hear when it becomes available.

Does it need an app?

No. Veyro One is designed to work as a simple standalone physical product.

Who is Veyro One for?

Veyro One is designed for studying, productivity, classrooms, offices, home use and anyone who wants a simpler way to visualize time.
